Male Reproductive System

Major organs and their functions.

Testes

The testes produce sperm cells and the hormone testosterone.

Epididymis

The epididymis is where sperm mature and are stored.

Vas Deferens

The vas deferens transports sperm from the epididymis toward the reproductive ducts.

Seminal Vesicles

These glands contribute fluid containing nutrients that support sperm.

Prostate Gland

The prostate contributes fluid to semen and surrounds part of the urethra.

Urethra

The urethra is a tube through which urine leaves the body. In males, it also carries semen during ejaculation.
