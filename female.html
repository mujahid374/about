Female Reproductive System

Major organs and their functions.

Ovaries

The ovaries produce ova (egg cells) and hormones such as estrogen and progesterone.

Fallopian Tubes

The fallopian tubes connect the ovaries with the uterus. Fertilization commonly occurs within a fallopian tube.

Uterus

The uterus is the organ where a fertilized egg can implant and where pregnancy develops.

Cervix

The cervix is the lower part of the uterus that opens into the vagina.

Vagina

The vagina is a muscular canal connecting the cervix with the outside of the body.
