The Process of Human Reproduction

1

Production of Gametes

The male reproductive system produces sperm cells, while the female reproductive system produces egg cells.

2

Fertilization

Fertilization occurs when a sperm cell fuses with an egg cell, forming a zygote.

3

Early Development

The zygote undergoes cell division and develops into an embryo.

4

Pregnancy

The developing embryo and fetus grow inside the uterus during pregnancy.
